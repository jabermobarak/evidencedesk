# Northstar Labs — Expense Policy

This is fictional demonstration data, written for EvidenceDesk. Monetary amounts are examples.

## Business travel

Business travel expenses require a receipt and a project code. The meal reimbursement limit is 2500 RUB per person per day. Hotel expenses above 12000 RUB per night require written approval from a department head before booking.

## Claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. The finance team processes approved expense claims within 10 business days. Personal entertainment and alcohol are not reimbursable.

## Equipment

Employees may request a home office equipment allowance of 15000 RUB once per year. Equipment purchases require manager approval before ordering. Purchased equipment remains company property.
