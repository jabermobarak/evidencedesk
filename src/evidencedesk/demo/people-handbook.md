# Northstar Labs — People Handbook

This is fictional demonstration data, written for EvidenceDesk. It is not employment advice.

## Time off

Employees receive 28 calendar days of annual leave per year. Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 14 days before the first day of leave. The line manager approves annual leave requests. Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the following year.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's approval. Remote workers must use company-managed devices with disk encryption and multi-factor authentication.

## New starters

New employees complete security awareness training within their first 7 days. The buddy program lasts for the first 30 days of employment. All new employees receive a company laptop before their first working day.
